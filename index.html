2004 年加入澳門博覽集團，至今逾二十年。累計參與超過十五屆澳門國際貿易投資展覽會（MIF），2015 至 2025 年連續十一年擔任承辦項目統籌；2021 年起主責澳門國際環保合作發展論壇及展覽（MIECF）。

2025 年出任副總經理，並兼任珠海澳博會展有限公司總經理，推進澳琴「一會展兩地」跨境會展模式，把澳門的國際會展標準帶入橫琴粵澳深度合作區。

2024 年起把 AI 系統性導入會展工作流程，建立 C.H.E. 人機協作會展管理體系：團隊人效提升 50%，新人培育週期由一個月縮短至一週。這個網站整理的，就是這些方法和成品。

Kelvin joined Macao Expo Group in 2004. He has participated in more than 15 editions of the Macao International Trade and Investment Fair (MIF), serving as lead project coordinator for 11 consecutive years (2015–2025), and has led the Macao International Environmental Co-operation Forum and Exhibition (MIECF) since 2021.

Promoted to Deputy General Manager in 2025, he also heads Zhuhai Macau Expo Co., Ltd., driving the Macao-Hengqin "One Event, Two Destinations" model that brings Macao's international exhibition standards into the Hengqin Guangdong-Macao In-depth Cooperation Zone.

Since 2024 he has embedded AI into exhibition workflows through the C.H.E. human-AI collaboration system, lifting team productivity by 50% and cutting new-staff onboarding from one month to one week. This site collects those methods and the work behind them.

01 · 方法論Method

AI 7A 成長模型The AI 7A Growth Model

很多人用過大量 AI，卻沒有留下什麼。7A 模型把學習順序倒轉過來：先建立判斷力，再沉澱知識，最後才談工具。Many people use plenty of AI yet keep little of it. The 7A model reverses the usual order: build judgement first, capture knowledge next, and only then add tools.

「存得住」和「能持續產生價值」，中間隔了一整個層級。"Storing something and getting lasting value from it are a whole level apart."

已發表於 LinkedIn 及公眾號「澳琴會展圈」。Published on LinkedIn and the WeChat account 澳琴會展圈.

  1. Alignment 認知對齊 — 先界定哪些事必須人做— define what must stay human
  2. Awareness 邊界覺察 — 知道何時可以相信它— know when to trust it
  3. Acquisition 自驅學習 — 由真實痛點驅動— learn from real pain points
  4. Action 場景動手 — 在真實工作流中試錯— iterate inside real workflows
  5. Archive 知識底座 — 素材可檢索、可調用— make material searchable and reusable
  6. Asset 數字資產 — 知識變成可複用的成品— turn knowledge into reusable outputs
  7. Augmentation 系統放大 — 工具，放在最後— tools come last
02 · 治理Governance

澳門會展業 AI 負責任應用與治理參考手冊Responsible AI in Macao's MICE Industry: A Reference Handbook

把 AI 應用按展會生命週期分級管理，並列出從業員的建議做與嚴禁做事項。手冊對應 ISO/IEC 42001、NIST AI RMF、澳門《個人資料保護法》及 UFI 指引。Rates AI use by stage of the event lifecycle and sets clear do's and don'ts for practitioners. Aligned with ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, Macao's Personal Data Protection Act and UFI guidance.

03 · 工具Tool

Booth Studio 展位設計工具Booth Designer

為第 31 屆 MIF 開發的標準展位展板設計工具。把現成提示詞改寫為會展場景，結合大會官方尺寸，一天內分兩期完成 2D 平面增拆、噴畫對位、3D 預覽及加租清單導出。A standard booth design tool built for the 31st MIF. An existing prompt was adapted to exhibition use and official booth specifications, delivering 2D layout editing, graphic placement, 3D preview and add-on equipment lists in two phases within one day.

同步建立提示詞庫及配色方案庫，把一次性產出變成可重用的數字資產。Reusable prompt and colour-palette libraries were set up at the same time, turning a one-off build into a lasting digital asset.
